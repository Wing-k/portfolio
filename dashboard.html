Personal Banking Dashboard

Personal Finance
Problem Hard to see where money goes each month without manually piecing together bank statements and categories.
Approach Built a dashboard tracking total income, expenses, and net savings at a glance — with monthly income vs. expense trends, income source breakdown, and expense distribution by category.
Tools
Tableau Excel
Impact Makes monthly financial health visible at a glance — surfacing spending patterns, income variability, and savings gaps without manual calculation.
Learned How to structure a personal finance view so the most important numbers (net savings) lead, with supporting breakdowns layered below — top-down information hierarchy.

Business Performance Dashboard

Sales & Profit
Problem Business data scattered across categories, regions, and time periods — difficult to identify which products, states, or months are driving or dragging performance.
Approach Built an interactive cross-filtered dashboard — selecting a state filters all other charts (sales by month, profit by month, discount trends, category breakdown, and product-level sales vs. profit) to show that state's full picture.
Tools
Tableau Excel
Impact Enables quick diagnosis of regional performance — surfaces which states are high-discount/low-profit and which categories drive the most margin, without needing a separate report per state.
Learned How to design for exploration — making charts interactive so stakeholders can self-serve answers, reducing the need to request custom cuts of data every time a question comes up.
